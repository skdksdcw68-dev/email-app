Maily

Email that sorts itself out.

Maily is an email app for iPhone. It reads your inbox the way you would — what needs an answer, what can wait, what is just noise — and it can write the replies with you.

Sorted on arrival

Every message lands in a category you control. Add your own and describe it in your own words.

Ask your inbox

"What did the landlord say about the deposit?" — answered from your own mail, with the message attached.

Replies in your voice

Drafts that sound like you, from how you already write. Nothing sends without you.

Gmail and IMAP

Connect a Google account, or any mail server. Several at once, kept apart.

What Maily does with your mail

Your messages are read on your phone and kept there. Maily's servers hold your settings and your subscription, never the contents of your mailbox. When a feature needs the assistant, the text of that message is sent to our AI provider to answer it and is not used to train anybody's model.

The whole of it is written down plainly in the privacy policy.
